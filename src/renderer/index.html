Supported SVG artwork

Paths (M/L/H/V/C/S/Q/T/Z), lines, polygons, polylines, rectangles, circles, and ellipses. Group transforms: translate, scale, rotate, matrix.

Export plain visible paths. Convert text and arcs to Bézier paths; remove styles, images, clipping, and references. Filled shapes become outlines. The longest dimension is scaled to Design size.

G-code review

XYZ ONLY

Open a file or paste commands, then review. No connection needed.

Supported G-code

G21, G90/G91, G0/G1 XYZ/F, G4 (up to 2 seconds), M400. First XY move must establish both X and Y plus Z, or follow an explicit Z lift. Feed rate 60–3000 mm/min. Maximum 256 KB and 5,000 commands. Comments are accepted.

Unsupported commands stop review with a line number. Laser, heater, extrusion, homing, offsets, and accessory commands are not imported. G0 is previewed as dashed travel and normalized to linear G1 for simulation. Source coordinates are used directly, independent of the drawing work origin.
